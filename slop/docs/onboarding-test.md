# Onboarding Test Script

Run this with one real person before launch.

1. Ask the tester to open the production URL without coaching.
2. They create an account.
3. They accept or decline analytics consent.
4. They run one text scan and explain what they think the score means.
5. They visit Billing and identify how to subscribe.
6. They find Help, Terms, Privacy, and Support.
7. Record where they hesitate, misunderstand the score, or fail to complete signup to activation.

Pass condition: tester reaches a completed scan without help and understands that SLOP scores are signals, not verdicts.
