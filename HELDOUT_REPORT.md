## Image Diagram Gate Empirical Investigation (Rejected Candidates)

Empirical evaluation of tighter authority gating for the diagram heuristic across the full 66-image evaluation benchmark (`golden_set/image/` + `heldout_set/image/` — 27 AI, 39 Human):

| Gate Candidate | Benchmark Accuracy | AI Recall | Human Specificity | Flips vs. Baseline | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Original 0.70 Binary Gate (Incumbent)** | **74.24% (49/66)** | **74.07% (20/27)** | **74.36% (29/39)** | Baseline (0 flips) | **Retained (Optimal)** |
| **Tighter 0.35 Binary Gate** | 72.73% (48/66) | 74.07% (20/27) | 71.79% (28/39) | 1 flip (`diagram_02` $\to$ False AI) | **REJECTED** (-1.51%) |
| **Three-Zone Graduated Gate (0.35–0.70)** | 72.73% (48/66) | 74.07% (20/27) | 71.79% (28/39) | 1 flip (`diagram_02` $\to$ False AI) | **REJECTED** (-1.51%) |

**Empirical Finding:**
The `-6.5` diagram weight is not an arbitrary heuristic override, but a legitimate learned correction fitted in `train_fusion.py` where $100\%$ of `is_diagram=1` training examples were human vector/technical diagrams. The Swin vision transformer has an inherent blind spot on vector diagrams due to the complete lack of physical camera sensor noise (PRNU/CFA), generating false-positive neural scores in the $0.65–0.70$ band. Both tighter candidate gates were **honestly rejected** because they recovered zero AI diagrams while creating a false positive on authentic technical drawings. The pipeline was reverted to the original 0.70 binary threshold.

## Image gate: in-sample inflation (measured) + CLIP probe v2 candidate
